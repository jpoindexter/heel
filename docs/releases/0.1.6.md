# Heel 0.1.6 — Photos

- Open **Photos** from Heel to choose, paste or drop images and preview them.
- Ask about a photo, read its text locally, crop, rotate, resize or remove its background.
- Make generative photo edits through your **Codex ChatGPT subscription**, with no API key.
- Review edits, undo changes and save a separate PNG copy to Desktop. Original files stay untouched.

From Heel 0.1.5, the update downloads automatically and offers **Restart to update**. You can also check under Settings → About. Older versions require the DMG once.

**Requirements and limits:** Apple silicon Mac. Generative edits require a current Codex CLI signed in with ChatGPT and image-generation access. Background removal requires macOS 14 or later. Working copies fit 2048 pixels; save edits before quitting. Local photo tools use the bundled native helper and do not invoke a compiler.

**Validation:** photo preview/edit/export/reopen and local text reading were exercised in the native app. Separate live checks exercised subscription vision/editing and local background removal. Iris’s Mac and Intel hardware have not been tested.
