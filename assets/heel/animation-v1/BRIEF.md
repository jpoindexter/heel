# Greg animation export

Outcome: full transparent animation sheets using the approved charcoal-grey Greg design, plus playable previews and timing metadata.

Target: this local repository. Keep Riff and all earlier Greg variants intact. No pet creation, replacement, activation, publishing, or VPN changes.

Identity: broad rounded head, small natural folded ears, slate/charcoal coat, dark nose and muzzle, white only under chin and on chest. Preserve the approved illustration style. No headphones, collar, scenery, detached effects, sparkles, motion lines, or sound.

Now: generate and inspect independent grounded animation strips.

Next: extract transparent frames, assemble the standard 9 animation rows, then generate and inspect 16 look directions.

Later: package six calm Heel states and the full Dot-format export; play the final encoded assets and record any remaining limitations.

Done evidence: correct frame counts, transparent unused cells, uncut silhouettes, meaningful movement, stable scale/baseline, correct look directions, saved validation reports, actual browser playback, and downloadable local files. Structural checks alone do not establish motion quality.

Discovery: checked skills.sh leaderboard and ran `npx skills find 'sprite animation'`. Results included PixiJS, Phaser, OpenAI sprite-pipeline, and several generation services. Plugin discovery returned Higgsfield/Figma; neither was needed. Selected the already-installed OpenAI Pets pipeline because it supplies the exact atlas geometry, extraction, timing, direction registration, and quality checks used by Riff.
